# Visual Identity Guidelines

> *"An identity is not built by repeating visual elements.
> It is built by making coherent decisions."*

---

# Preface

The visual identity of Projet Atlas is not intended to create a fashionable or visually impressive product.

Its purpose is to faithfully express the philosophy of the project.

These guidelines do not define a set of immutable rules.
They define a common direction.

Every contributor remains free to create, propose and innovate.

However, every visual decision should naturally reinforce the identity of the project instead of weakening it.

This document therefore exists to explain **why** our visual choices exist before explaining **what** they are.

If, one day, a colour, an illustration or an animation needs to evolve, its purpose should remain unchanged.

The objective is not consistency for its own sake.

The objective is that Projet Atlas remains recognisable because every decision comes from the same philosophy.

---

# 1. Our Philosophy

Visual identity is not decoration.

It is a language.

Every colour, every illustration, every animation, every icon and every layout conveys an intention.

Nothing should exist simply because it looks attractive.

Everything should exist because it communicates something meaningful.

For this reason, visual decisions are never evaluated with the question:

> "Does it look good?"

Instead, they are evaluated with another question:

> "Does it express the values of Projet Atlas?"

Only then do aesthetic considerations become relevant.

---

# Our Mission

The purpose of our visual identity is to make people feel that Projet Atlas is:

• calm

• trustworthy

• organised

• accessible

• timeless

• human

Never:

• luxurious

• aggressive

• overloaded

• intimidating

• unnecessarily technical

Our identity should inspire confidence before admiration.
